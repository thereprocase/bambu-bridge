FAQ

Bambu Bridge · Manual · FAQ
Do I have to assign every spool?
Bambu Lab spools with a tag assign themselves when the AMS reads the tag. Every other spool needs one tap in the assign sheet when you load it. A print from a slot with no spool is still printed; its grams wait on a card for you afterwards.
What happens to grams while Spoolman is down?
The bridge keeps counting and keeps trying for six hours, writing only the difference each time. If Spoolman stays away past that, the print becomes a card: "40 g for #5 · Spoolman didn't answer", and you charge it when Spoolman is back.
Can the bridge charge the wrong spool?
If you swap two identical untagged spools without telling the app, yes. Fix it with Wrong spool? on the print; the bridge moves the grams and the history. Tagged spools are keyed on their tag, so a swap is noticed.
Why does a slot's swatch have a ring?
The fill is the spool's colour in Spoolman; the ring is what the printer declares for that slot when the two differ. A refill backup does that on purpose; a leftover declaration from an earlier spool is harmless and goes away when the bridge syncs the colour.
Why no bar on some spools?
The bar is Spoolman's figure for an assigned spool. An unassigned slot shows the printer's own estimate, which only Bambu tagged spools report.
Can I use a tagged spool in a refill backup?
Yes, as the one the other slot copies. The tagged slot is never written. Two tagged spools can't back each other up.
Can a backup cross materials?
No. PLA and PETG never back each other up; the sheet only offers same-material slots. Product, temperatures and colour are aligned by the backup; the material type is yours to match.
Does Read tag work during a print?
No. The printer reads tags when it's idle; the buttons wait and the card says so.
What does the bridge store about spools?
Which spool is in which slot, which tag belongs to which spool, the per-print ledger of grams written, and any active backup. Spool records themselves are Spoolman's.
Will an update lose anything?
The bridge's config, job history and spool assignments survive an update; the update script backs the database up first. A stored refill backup survives too and re-confirms from the printer's next report (see Refill backups).
Can the app run a print from Orca's profile?
The Print tab starts files already on the printer's storage, single-filament. Multi-filament jobs are started from the slicer through the bridge; they show on Status and are charged the same way.
Is anything sent to Bambu's cloud?
No. The printer runs in LAN-Only Mode and the bridge talks to it directly on your network.
